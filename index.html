        Privacy

        Recipes are saved on this device. If sync is set up, they are also stored in your own Google Sheet, reached through the web app link: anyone with that link can read and change the recipes, so only share it with each other. Nothing else is sent anywhere, and there is no tracking. To delete everything, delete the recipes here and the Sheet in Google Drive.
